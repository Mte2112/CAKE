About

Addressing climate change requires a collective effort, bridging science with societal domains such as policy, law, and engineering. However, interdisciplinary collaboration currently lacks needed support, and those engaged in climate-related fields often work in isolation. The purpose of CAKE is to break down silos and foster collaboration across disciplines oriented around climate, energy, and the environment. By connecting academics, professionals, and local communities, we create a space for open dialogue, learning, and networking. Our goal is to drive impactful climate action through cultivating cross-disciplinary relationships, knowledge-sharing, and community engagement.
