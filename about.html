Our Mission

Climate change is already harming people and the environment, and the damage will only increase. Overcoming this challenge requires united action on mitigation and adaptation. CAKE brings together scholars, industry professionals, and local community members to build strong partnerships for targeted, interdisciplinary climate solutions.
