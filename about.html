About CAKE

Building bridges across disciplines for climate action

Our Mission

Climate change is already affecting society and the environment, and its impacts will get worse. This demands collaborative solutions to mitigate and adapt to climate change. CAKE provides a platform where science, policy, law, engineering, trades, art, and more come together.

We connect scholars, professionals, and community members in meaningful exchange—fostering the relationships needed to drive effective climate action.
